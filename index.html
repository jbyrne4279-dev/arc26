The daily loop

How a day works

The same short loop every day, resetting at 5am.

Step 01

Set your tasks

Pick a few daily tasks. Each is photo-verified, lock-based, or a simple check-off.

Step 02

Lock in

Shield your apps with Screen Time. Open one and hit a block screen — until every task's done.

Step 03

Settle the score

At 5am the day is graded. Clear it — gain ELO and extend your streak. Miss it — lose both.

Features

Everything that keeps you honest.

Five systems. Zero ways to fake it.

Verified tasks

Tasks you prove, not just tick.

No "mark as done." Snap a live photo — verified in seconds.

  • Live capture only. No camera roll.
  • Judged against your task.
  • Photo's checked, then gone.
Lock Mode

A lock that holds until the work is done.

Screen Time shields your apps until the tasks are done. No timer to wait out.

  • Task-bound, not timed.
  • Blocked apps hit a wall.
  • Earn minutes for breaks.
The exit gate

Quitting early means making your case.

Try to bail and a coach argues back. The exit only shows after the verdict.

  • Real emergencies still pass.
Earn time back
