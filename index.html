The cost

The apps eating your day
cost you six years.

Six years of an average lifetime, handed to a feed one swipe at a time. Motivation runs out by 9am. What you need is a system that doesn't — one that makes the easy path the disciplined one, and puts a wall in front of the rest.

How it works

Three steps. No negotiation.

Set the bar, lock in, and let the day settle the score.

Step 01

Pick your tasks.

Choose a small set of daily tasks. Most are verified with a live camera photo — no "mark as done", no talking yourself out of it.

Step 02

Lock in.

Locking shields your chosen apps until every task on today's list is complete. It isn't a timer you wait out. The only way past it is through it.

Step 03

Climb the ladder.

Clear the day and earn ELO. Miss it and lose it. Ten ranks stand between Bronze and Champion, and every day moves your number one way or the other.

What's inside

Built to be hard to fool.

Every feature exists to close a loophole you'd otherwise use on yourself.

Photo proof

The camera opens live and judges the shot against your task. No camera roll, no old photos, no shortcuts. You prove it or it doesn't count.

A lock you can't scroll past

Lock Mode is task-bound, not timed. Open a shielded app and a block screen stands in the way instead. It lifts when the work is done — not a minute before.

The Emergency Coach

Try to quit early and it argues back. Out-argue a drill-sergeant coach across five rounds before the exit even appears. Most people put the phone down instead.

A ranked ELO ladder

Every action moves a real number. Full days climb, missed days fall, and ten ranks — Bronze to Champion — turn discipline into something you can watch rise.

Earn your minutes back

Win the daily brain game and bank reward time. Spend it in short, capped windows — the shield drops, then comes straight back. Downtime you earned, not stole.

You're not doing this alone

Post wins, struggles and questions to a community grinding the same arc. Pick a handle, drop the highlight reel, and show up honestly.
