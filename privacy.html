Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This policy explains what Winter Arc 26 (“the app”, “we”, “us”), operated by [YOUR COMPANY / LEGAL ENTITY], collects, why, and what you can do about it. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Winter Arc 26 is an iOS discipline app. Contact us at privacy@winterarc26.app with any privacy question or request.

What we collect

  • Account & identity. When you sign in with Apple we receive a user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and a relay email address. You may also choose a public @username and display name.
  • Your activity. The tasks you configure, completions, streaks, ELO, rank, reward minutes, and related progress, so the app can keep score and sync across sessions.
  • Task proof photos. When a task requires photo verification, the photo you capture is transmitted for automated checking (see “Photo verification” below).
  • Community content. Posts, replies and reactions you create are stored and shown to other users under your chosen username.
  • Purchases. Subscription status is managed through Apple and our billing provider (RevenueCat). We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Device & diagnostic data. Basic technical information needed to operate the service and diagnose problems.

Photo verification — important

Photos captured for task proof are sent off your device to our cloud service and a third-party AI vision provider, which judges the photo against the task's criteria and returns a pass/fail result. Proof photos are used only for that check and are not stored by us after verification, not posted to the community, and not used to build a profile of you. Because the photo leaves your device for processing, do not capture anything in the frame you would not want transmitted for automated review.

Screen Time & app blocking

The lock feature uses Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls framework. The selection of apps you choose to block is handled on your device by Apple's framework. We do not receive a list of the specific apps you use or block.

How we use your data

  • To run the core features: tasks, verification, scoring, the ladder, lock sessions, rewards and the community.
  • To keep the community safe. Posts and replies are screened by an automated moderation service and a profanity filter; content that violates our rules may be removed automatically.
  • To manage your subscription and provide support.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the service.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, under agreements that limit their use of it:

  • Apple — sign-in, Screen Time, and subscription billing.
  • Google Firebase — authentication, database and backend hosting.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • An AI provider — photo-proof verification, community moderation and the in-app coach.

We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep account and activity data for as long as your account is active. Proof photos are not retained after verification. Community content remains visible until you or we remove it. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise data we no longer need, subject to legal obligations.

Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email privacy@winterarc26.app. You can stop all collection by deleting the app and your account.

Children

Changes
